while verifying that every known deployed production writer retains revoked
authority and that the current key was not distributed into the selected project
credential. This relies on the completed known-source audit and continuous
operator/deployment/environment-copy hold, not hypothetical global OIDC absence.

deployment or environment copy is allowed during this interval.
